## What is still unproven when you finish

- ⚠️⚠️ **No email has been seen to arrive.** You are testing the product, not the delivery. The transport is
  installed and the failure path is proven loud, but until the Postmark sender is verified and the token is
  set, nothing reaches an inbox — and today a send **silently appears to succeed**.
- ⚠️ `/admin/login` has **no account**, so its mandatory 2FA has still never fired on this host.
- ⚠️ The site stays **parked**, and is now **also locked**. You are reaching it with your password plus
  your bypass; the public is stopped by the lock before Laravel even runs.
- ⚠️ **THE QUEUE IS PAUSED, NOT BROKEN.** 🔬 Re-proven 2026-09-23 by reading the cron's own command line:
  a worker runs every minute on the correct PHP 8.4 binary (`queue:work --stop-when-empty --max-time=55`).
  While the site is parked it exits immediately, so nothing drains; the minute you unpark, it drains. Right
  now `jobs` is **0** and `failed_jobs` is **0**.
- ⚠️ **Mail still vanishes, and that is separate from the queue.** `MAIL_MAILER=log` on production, so a
  send is written to the log rather than delivered — and Laravel's log mailer writes at `debug` level while
  the app's log level is higher, so it is **discarded without even being recorded**. Nothing in this walk
  depends on an email arriving; do not read a quiet inbox as a bug you have found.
