## ⚠️ WHAT THIS AUDIT FOUND THAT IS NOT A COPY PROBLEM

Two findings are **product defects**, discovered by auditing marketing copy. Neither is fixed here — both are
outside this gate — and both are reported rather than papered over.

### 🔴 1. NINETEEN OPERATOR SCREENS CARRY NO PERMISSION GATE, AND THREE OF THEM RENDER MONEY

The design claims *"a housekeeper sees rooms, not revenue."* The role catalogue models that intent correctly
— `Housekeeping` holds `units.view`, `housekeeping.*` and `schedule.view` and nothing else, and the dedicated
Reports, Insights and Guests screens correctly refuse it. ⚠️ **But the operator dashboard is not gated.**
`routes/web.php:620` registers `/app/dashboard` with no `can:` middleware, and
`OperatorDashboard.php:123` computes "Revenue today" — settled payments minus refunds — unconditionally.
Enumerating every `/app` GET route and filtering for an absent `Authorize` middleware entry finds **nineteen
ungated screens, three of which render money**.

⚠️ The marketing sentence was cut back to the two clauses that *are* enforced. **The gate itself is the
founder's call and a separate change.**

### 🔴 2. A CODE COMMENT DEFERS A GDPR CASE TO A MECHANISM THAT DOES NOT EXIST

`Guest::anonymize()` — the certified erasure path — states at `app/Models/Guest.php:349-351` that abandoned
booking quotes *"are cleared by the scheduled retention purge, not from here."* ⚠️ **There is no scheduled
retention purge.** The eight scheduled commands are enumerated in `routes/console.php:15-72` and none touches
`BookingQuote`. A prospective guest's encrypted email, phone and snapshot are retained **indefinitely** and
are **unreachable by erasure**, while the code reads as though the case were handled.

⚠️ This is the most dangerous shape a gap takes: not an absence, but an absence with a comment saying it is
covered. Full detail in `docs/legal/DATA-INVENTORY.md`.
