**⚠️⚠️ THIS PASS IS INTERACTIVE. ⚠️ HE MUST BE SITTING THERE. ⚠️ IT RUNS IN A VISIBLE BROWSER SO HE CAN WATCH AND APPROVE EVERY STEP.**
**⚠️⚠️ HE LOGS IN. YOU NEVER DO. ⚠️ NO PASSWORD, NO 2FA CODE, NO SESSION TOKEN EVER PASSES THROUGH YOU, A COMMAND, A FILE, OR A LOG.**
**⚠️ THE MACHINE'S JOB HERE IS ONE THING AND IT IS THE THING MACHINES ARE BEST AT: ⚠️⚠️ **COPYING A 400-CHARACTER STRING WITHOUT A SINGLE TYPO.**

## ⚠️ #0 — ⚠️⚠️ **FIND OUT WHAT IS ALREADY THERE BEFORE TOUCHING ANYTHING**

- ⚠️⚠️ **THIS DOMAIN MAY ALREADY CARRY HIS WORKING EMAIL. ⚠️⚠️ IF HE RECEIVES MAIL AT `vyrobel.com` TODAY, A CARELESS RECORD CHANGE **BREAKS HIS OWN INBOX**, AND HE WILL NOT FIND OUT FOR HOURS.**
- ⚠️ **DO NOT ASK HIM WHO HIS DNS PROVIDER IS — ⚠️ **MEASURE IT**: ⚠️ `dig NS vyrobel.com` NAMES THE NAMESERVERS, AND THE NAMESERVERS NAME THE PROVIDER.**
- ⚠️ **THEN READ AND REPORT, BEFORE ANY CHANGE, EVERY ONE OF THESE:**
  - ⚠️ `dig MX vyrobel.com` — ⚠️⚠️ **IF MX RECORDS EXIST, HE HAS LIVE EMAIL HERE. ⚠️ SAY SO LOUDLY. ⚠️ DO NOT TOUCH MX AT ANY POINT IN THIS PASS.**
  - ⚠️ `dig TXT vyrobel.com` — ⚠️⚠️ **LOOK FOR AN EXISTING SPF (`v=spf1`). ⚠️⚠️ TWO SPF RECORDS ON ONE DOMAIN IS A HARD FAILURE — ⚠️ AN EXISTING ONE IS **MERGED**, NEVER JOINED BY A SECOND.**
  - ⚠️ `dig TXT _dmarc.vyrobel.com` — ⚠️ report the policy if one exists. ⚠️ **DO NOT CHANGE DMARC IN THIS PASS.**
  - ⚠️ any existing `pm._domainkey` or Return-Path CNAME from a previous attempt.
- ⚠️⚠️ **WRITE THE WHOLE "BEFORE" STATE DOWN AND SHOW IT TO HIM. ⚠️ IT IS THE ROLLBACK.**

## ⚠️ #1 — ⚠️ POSTMARK: **HE SIGNS IN, YOU DRIVE THE FORM**

- ⚠️ **OPEN A VISIBLE BROWSER. ⚠️ NAVIGATE TO POSTMARK. ⚠️ **THEN STOP AND HAND HIM THE KEYBOARD.** ⚠️ He signs in — or creates the account if he does not yet have one.**
- ⚠️ **ONCE HE IS IN: ⚠️ SENDER SIGNATURES / DOMAINS → ADD DOMAIN → `vyrobel.com` → **DKIM**.**
- ⚠️⚠️ **POSTMARK NOW SHOWS TWO THINGS. ⚠️ CAPTURE THEM **PROGRAMMATICALLY — READ THE DOM, DO NOT RETYPE AND DO NOT TRANSCRIBE FROM A SCREENSHOT**:**
  - ⚠️ **A DKIM `TXT` RECORD** — host like `pm._domainkey`, value several hundred characters.
  - ⚠️ **A RETURN-PATH `CNAME`** — host like `pm-bounces`, pointing at Postmark.
- ⚠️⚠️ **AND CHECK ACCOUNT STATE WHILE YOU ARE THERE: ⚠️ A NEW POSTMARK ACCOUNT MAY BE **PENDING APPROVAL** UNTIL A USE CASE IS DESCRIBED, AND MAY BE RESTRICTED TO APPROVED RECIPIENTS UNTIL THEN. ⚠️⚠️ **IF SO, THAT IS A SECOND WAITING PERIOD AND HE NEEDS TO KNOW TONIGHT, NOT TOMORROW.**
- ⚠️ **CONFIRM WHICH MESSAGE STREAM IS TRANSACTIONAL. ⚠️ CONFIRMATIONS AND INVITES BELONG THERE, NOT ON A BROADCAST STREAM.**

## ⚠️ #2 — ⚠️⚠️ THE DNS PROVIDER: **THE ONE STEP WHERE A MACHINE BEATS A HUMAN**

- ⚠️ **NAVIGATE TO THE PROVIDER FOUND IN #0. ⚠️ **STOP. HE SIGNS IN.** ⚠️ Then resume.**
- ⚠️⚠️ **ADD ONLY WHAT POSTMARK ASKED FOR, PASTED FROM WHAT YOU CAPTURED — ⚠️ **NEVER RETYPED, NEVER SHORTENED, NEVER "CLEANED UP".** ⚠️ One wrong character in a DKIM value fails silently and looks exactly like propagation delay for days.**
- ⚠️⚠️ **WATCH FOR THE PROVIDER'S OWN HELPFULNESS — ⚠️ IT IS THE MAIN SOURCE OF ERROR HERE:**
  - ⚠️ **SOME PROVIDERS APPEND THE DOMAIN AUTOMATICALLY.** ⚠️ Entering `pm._domainkey.vyrobel.com` may become `pm._domainkey.vyrobel.com.vyrobel.com`. ⚠️ **CHECK WHAT THE SAVED RECORD ACTUALLY SAYS.**
  - ⚠️ **SOME SPLIT OR QUOTE LONG TXT VALUES.** ⚠️ Report exactly what was stored.
  - ⚠️ **SOME REFUSE A CNAME WHERE OTHER RECORDS EXIST.** ⚠️ Say so rather than deleting anything to make room.
- ⚠️⚠️ **IF AN SPF ALREADY EXISTS: ⚠️ **STOP AND SHOW HIM THE EXACT MERGE YOU PROPOSE BEFORE SAVING IT.** ⚠️ Adding Postmark to an existing SPF is one token; ⚠️ **getting it wrong silently breaks whatever already sends as this domain.**
- ⚠️⚠️ **NEVER DELETE OR EDIT AN EXISTING RECORD WITHOUT SHOWING HIM AND GETTING A YES OUT LOUD. ⚠️ ADDING IS SAFE. ⚠️ CHANGING IS NOT.**

## ⚠️ #3 — ⚠️⚠️ **READ THE RECORDS BACK** — ⚠️ THE RULE THE INVITE BUG BOUGHT, APPLIED TO DNS

- ⚠️⚠️ **A SAVED FORM IS NOT A PUBLISHED RECORD. ⚠️ **A WRITE IS PROVEN BY READING THE VALUE BACK.** ⚠️ THAT RULE COST THIS PROJECT A SILENTLY-DROPPED COLUMN THIS MORNING — ⚠️ IT APPLIES HERE UNCHANGED.**
- ⚠️ **`dig TXT pm._domainkey.vyrobel.com` AND `dig CNAME <return-path>.vyrobel.com`, AND **DIFF WHAT IS PUBLISHED AGAINST WHAT POSTMARK ASKED FOR, CHARACTER BY CHARACTER.**
- ⚠️⚠️ **AND GIVE THIS ITS THIRD VERDICT, BECAUSE IT WILL NEED ONE: ⚠️ **AN EMPTY ANSWER MINUTES AFTER SAVING IS PROPAGATION, NOT FAILURE.** ⚠️⚠️ REPORT **PUBLISHED AND MATCHING** / **PUBLISHED AND DIFFERENT** / **NOT YET VISIBLE** — ⚠️ AND NEVER COLLAPSE THE THIRD INTO EITHER OF THE OTHERS.**
- ⚠️ **QUERY AN AUTHORITATIVE NAMESERVER DIRECTLY AS WELL AS A PUBLIC RESOLVER. ⚠️ **AUTHORITATIVE ANSWERS IMMEDIATELY; A PUBLIC RESOLVER LAGS.** ⚠️ Different answers from the two is propagation, and saying which you asked is the difference between a measurement and a guess.**

## ⚠️ #4 — ⚠️ VERIFY AT POSTMARK, AND SAY WHAT HAPPENS NEXT

- ⚠️ **CLICK VERIFY IN POSTMARK. ⚠️ **IF IT FAILS MINUTES AFTER SAVING, THAT IS EXPECTED AND IT IS NOT AN ERROR.** ⚠️ Say so plainly and give him a realistic time to retry.**
- ⚠️ **WHEN IT VERIFIES, TELL HIM THE EXACT `MAIL_FROM_ADDRESS` THAT IS NOW LEGAL — ⚠️⚠️ **A FROM-ADDRESS THAT DOES NOT MATCH THE VERIFIED SENDER IS REJECTED, AND THAT IS THE ONE FAILURE MODE WE COULD NOT TEST WITHOUT A TOKEN.**
- ⚠️ **THEN STOP. ⚠️⚠️ **DO NOT SEND A TEST EMAIL IN THIS PASS.** ⚠️ THE TOKEN IS HIS TO TYPE AND THE SENDING PASS IS ITS OWN.**
